# How to Track an Evri Parcel: Step-by-Step Guide

Tracking an Evri parcel is straightforward once you have the right reference. Use this guide whether your package is coming from a high-street retailer, an online shop, or a friend who dropped it at a ParcelShop.

## What you need

- A **16-digit Evri tracking number**, or  
- An **8-digit calling card number** (left after a missed delivery), or  
- A ParcelShop receipt number if you are chasing a drop-off  

Retailers usually email or text the 16-character code when the parcel is despatched.

## Method 1 — Track on everitracking.com (Cainiao live)

1. Open [everitracking.com](/).  
2. Paste the full tracking number (or calling card) into the tracker.  
3. Tap **Track** and read the milestone timeline powered by Cainiao.  
4. Optional: choose Evri in the carrier picker if several networks look similar.  

## Method 2 — Calling card after a missed delivery

If the courier left a card:

1. Find the **8-digit** number printed on the card.  
2. Enter it in the tracker on this site.  
3. Check the next delivery attempt or divert to a neighbour / safe place if available.  

## Tracking a return

Returns use the same 16-character code. If you printed a label on a **Print in ParcelShop** device, use the long number under the printed label.
