# How to Divert Your Evri Parcel to a Neighbour or Safe Place

If you will not be home for an Evri delivery, diverting early keeps parcels secure and avoids a missed attempt.

## How diversion works

1. Track your parcel on [everitracking.com](/) with the **16-digit tracking number**.  
2. Use your **delivery postcode** with the diversion tools shown for your service when available.  
3. Review the delivery options shown.  

Depending on what the sender selected, you may be able to divert to:

- A **safe place**  
- A **neighbour**  
- **Neighbour only**  

If the sender requested a **household signature**, diversion is usually **not** available.

## Won’t be in at the expected time

You can divert at many points on the journey — not only on delivery day. Choosing a neighbour or safe place early reduces the chance of a calling card.

## Changing the delivery address

Most retailers manage full address changes themselves. Contact your **sender or retailer** first. If you only need a nearby alternative, divert to a neighbour or safe place instead of changing the address.

## Collecting from a ParcelShop or depot

If your parcel is with a **local courier** for home delivery, you generally **cannot** collect it from a ParcelShop or depot. The courier will make **up to 3 attempts**. Use divert or safe-place options if you will miss them.

## Accessibility delivery instructions

Recipients can often share courier preferences such as:

- A little **extra time** to get to the door  
- **Ring the doorbell** instead of knocking  
- **Avoid blocking** doorways or entrances when placing the parcel  

Add these via Evri My Places / app / the link in your delivery confirmation when available.
