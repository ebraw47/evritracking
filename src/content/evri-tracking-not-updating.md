# Evri Tracking Not Updating? Causes and What to Do

Quiet gaps in Evri tracking are common — especially between depot scans and the morning of delivery. Here is how to tell a normal pause from a problem.

## Normal reasons tracking pauses

- Parcel waiting in a busy depot during peak periods  
- Overnight linehaul between hubs with few public scans  
- Courier route not yet started for the day  
- Divert or safe-place change being processed  
- Weekend or bank-holiday sorting delays  

The package can still be moving even when the website looks quiet. Cainiao only shows scans that networks have published.

## When to worry

Contact the **sender** (retailer) if:

- There has been **no first scan** for several days after “dispatched”  
- Tracking has been stuck **over 7 days** with no new events after the parcel was with Evri  
- Status shows delivered but you have nothing and neighbours were not asked  

## Checklist before you escalate

1. **Re-paste** the full 16-digit number (or 8-digit calling card) into [everitracking.com](/).  
2. Confirm you are not mixing order ID with tracking ID.  
3. Check spam for newer retailer emails.  
4. Ask neighbours / check safe places if a delivery attempt was made.  

## Missed delivery

Couriers often make **up to 3 attempts**. Use divert options or the calling card number to stay ahead of the next visit. See [how to divert your Evri parcel](/how-to-divert-evri-parcel).
