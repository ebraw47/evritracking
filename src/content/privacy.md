# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how **everitracking.com** (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use https://everitracking.com and related pages.

## Who we are

everitracking.com is an independent package tracking website. We are not affiliated with Evri Limited. Contact: [contact@everitracking.com](mailto:contact@everitracking.com).

## Information we collect

- **Tracking numbers you submit** — processed via Cainiao to return shipment status; we do not require an account.  
- **Contact emails** — if you write to us.  
- **Technical data** — IP address, browser type, and similar analytics signals when analytics/ads scripts are enabled.  

We do not sell personal information.

## How we use information

- To provide tracking results  
- To reply to support messages  
- To improve site performance and content  
- To measure traffic when analytics is configured  

## Third parties

Tracking lookups call Cainiao’s tracking API. Analytics or advertising partners (if enabled via environment variables) may set cookies according to their policies.

## Your choices

- Request correction or deletion of personal data you sent us by emailing [contact@everitracking.com](mailto:contact@everitracking.com)  
- Use browser controls to block non-essential cookies  

## Retention

We keep correspondence only as long as needed to respond and operate the site. Tracking lookups are processed for the request and not stored as customer accounts.

## Updates

We may update this policy; related legal pages will change when we do.
