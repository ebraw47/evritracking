## What Is Evri Tracking?

Evri tracking is the process of following a UK (or Evri-network) parcel from drop-off or courier collection through sorting, out-for-delivery, and doorstep delivery using a unique tracking reference. After you place an order with a retailer that ships with Evri — or send a parcel yourself — you receive a tracking code so you can follow progress online.

Typical journey stages include:

- Order received / collected — the parcel enters the Evri network  
- At depot / in transit — sorting and linehaul between hubs  
- Out for delivery — with your local courier  
- Delivered / left in a safe place — signed, neighbour, or safe-place drop  
- Calling card left — a missed attempt with an 8-digit card number to re-track  

[evritracking](/) focuses on the tracking number itself so you can see progress in one place with **real-time Cainiao** lookups.

## How Evri Parcel Tracking Works

### When tracking becomes available

An Evri number often starts returning events after the first ParcelShop scan, locker drop, or courier collection. Until then, your retailer email may only show “dispatched.” That gap of hours to a day is common.

### 16-digit codes vs calling cards

Most Evri UK parcels use a **16-character** tracking code. If a courier tried to deliver and you were out, they may leave a **calling card** with an **8-digit** number — enter that in the tracker instead.

## How to Find Your Evri Tracking Number

1. Open the shipping or dispatch email/SMS from your retailer or Evri.  
2. Locate the **16-digit tracking number**.  
3. If you have a calling card from a missed delivery, use the **8-digit** number on the card.  
4. Copy the full number — do not use the order ID alone.  
5. Paste it into the tracker at the top of this page.

If nothing returns yet, the sender may not have handed the parcel over, or the first scan has not posted. Double-check for transposed characters.

### What slows UK delivery visibility

- Late retailer handoff or ParcelShop backlog  
- Busy depot sorting during peak seasons  
- Courier attempt windows when nobody is home  
- Holds when a signature or special instruction is required  
- Gaps while a diverted parcel is re-routed  

Once a parcel is out for delivery you usually cannot speed the courier’s route. Message the sender early if the first scan never appears after several days.

## Common Evri Tracking Statuses

Status text comes from ParcelShops, depots, and couriers — so wording varies. Here is what recipients see most often:

- Collected / accepted — parcel in the Evri network  
- At depot / in transit — moving between hubs  
- Out for delivery — with your local courier (often with a time window)  
- Delivered / left in safe place — completed attempt  
- Calling card left / we called — missed delivery; use the 8-digit card to track again  
- Diverted — redirected to neighbour, safe place, ParcelShop, or locker  

Quiet periods between these events are normal. If nothing moves for an unusually long time, see [Evri tracking not updating](/evri-tracking-not-updating).
